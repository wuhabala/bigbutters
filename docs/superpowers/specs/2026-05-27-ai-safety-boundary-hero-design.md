# AI Safety Boundary Hero Image Design

## Goal

Create one wide cover image for the `AI 安全边界` research series. The deliverable is a standalone asset saved to `pic/ai-safety-boundary-hero.png`; wiring it into the Astro content or layout is outside this task.

## Approved Direction

Use a text-forward, raw neo-expressionist graffiti-painting composition suited to a web hero. The image should feel urgent and handmade while leaving the core scrawled words visually recognizable.

## Visual Specification

- Canvas ratio: `1200:628` (`1.91:1`), horizontal hero.
- Ground: saturated yellow close to `#f5d040`.
- Mark-making: bold black ink strokes and an uneven hand-drawn black border.
- Upper-left motif: rough graffiti crown.
- Required written marks: `"A.I."`, `"SAFETY"`, `"RISK"`, and `"2026"`, overlapped at deliberate angles and prioritized for legibility.
- Central tension: a wildly sketched neural-network form crossed out in red.
- Directional accent: one cobalt-blue arrow piercing through the composition.
- Mood: energetic, naive, confrontational, suitable for an AI safety research landing page.

## Generation Approach

Generate a single PNG asset using the built-in image generation path. The prompt will preserve the supplied composition and palette while emphasizing readable hand-scrawled required words over incidental decorative writing.

## Acceptance Checks

- The saved asset exists at `pic/ai-safety-boundary-hero.png`.
- It is a wide raster image appropriate for hero use and close to the requested `1200:628` proportion.
- Yellow ground, black strokes/frame/crown, red crossed-out network, and blue arrow are all visible.
- All four required words or marks are present and recognizable.
- No application files are modified to reference the new asset.
